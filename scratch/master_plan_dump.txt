# **1. Executive Summary** 
  
This document is the master development plan for a three-bot algorithmic trading system. Each bot has been critically assessed, a targeted enrichment plan devised for each individually, and a phased roadmap established that culminates in a unified single-process architecture. The plan proceeds in four phases: individual enrichment, individual conviction testing, cross-validation, and finally unified architecture construction.

# **2. Critical Assessment — Each Model** 
  
## **2.1 SuperTrend Bot v2.2** 
  
| **5.5** | **Current live survival probability — Strategy: 7/10 │ Production readiness: 4/10** | 
| --- | --- | 
  
**What it gets right:** 
  
- Strategy class is sound — M30 trend following has decades of independently verified positive expectancy across liquid markets. 
  
- K-Means optimal factor selection is a genuine innovation over fixed-parameter SuperTrend systems. 
  
- SI score and state machine (INCUBATING → CONFIRMED → DECAYING → DEAD) is the best position management architecture of the three bots. 
  
- Bar-aware cache is production-grade engineering — eliminates ~59× redundant ATR computations per M30 bar. 
  
- Dynamic incubation bars per regime shows genuine market awareness. 
  
**Critical failures blocking live deployment:** 
  
- No failover mechanism — Python crash leaves positions unmanaged with only a hard SL. This is not a theoretical risk; it is a certainty over time. 
  
- Equity curve filter is disabled by default — the code is written correctly but the safety mechanism is turned off. 
  
- SI weights are not tuned per symbol — cluster=0.40, regime=0.25, adx=0.20, vol=0.15 are the same defaults across all instruments. 
  
- Volume component uses tick volume. On EURUSDm during Asian and overnight sessions, tick volume correlation degrades significantly. 
  
- K-Means uses last 100 bars for factor selection — regime transitions create a 10–20 bar lag window of mismatched factor after every major regime change. 
  
- incubation_bars_trending = 1 means active SL management starts after just 30 minutes — too fast for an M30 strategy to breathe. 
  
## **2.2 CAB Watcher v16.3 + MQL5 Watchdog** 
  
| **6.0** | **Current live survival probability — Watcher architecture: 8/10 │ Management logic: 5/10** | 
| --- | --- | 
  
**What it gets right:** 
  
- Most production-ready infrastructure of the three — MQL5 two-tier heartbeat failover (60s warn, 120s hard) is essential engineering. 
  
- Spread guards and STOPS_LEVEL checks on every modify prevent silent broker rejections. 
  
- Percentile regime classifier (ADX/ATR/body three-signal) is scale-invariant and zero-dependency. 
  
- Fluid matrix concept (Harvester / Protector / Reaper) is internally consistent with a clear management philosophy. 
  
**Critical failures and limitations:** 
  
- REAPER at -0.5R in TRENDING is the most dangerous setting. XAUUSD routinely retraces 0.4–0.8R in a normal pullback before continuing. This will mechanically cut correct trades. 
  
- HARVESTER is all-or-nothing at 2R — no partial close, no runner. Positions that have further to go after EXHAUSTION is detected are closed completely. 
  
- Between milestone events (Protector / Harvester / Reaper), there is zero active management. A position can decay from 1.3R to 0.5R and the watcher does nothing. 
  
- CAB is a watcher only — it cannot assess entry quality. Its survival rating is wholly dependent on whatever generates the positions it watches. 
  
- No session awareness — manages XAUUSD positions identically at 2:00 AM Asian session and 14:00 London/NY overlap. 
  
## **2.3 Ghost Grid Reversion — Ghost Sniper v5.0 + Watcher v3.0** 
  
| **?** | **Cannot score fairly — the watcher personality contradicts the strategy philosophy** | 
| --- | --- | 
  
The critical finding from the full design discussion is that the Ghost Sniper as currently deployed is NOT the strategy originally conceived. Understanding this distinction is the single most important insight in this document. 
  
**The original concept — Ghost Cache Grid:** 
  
- Run a virtual (ghost) grid in memory — no real trades — accumulating positions against the market move. 
  
- Detect market exhaustion by tracking where the nth virtual layer would have been placed. 
  
- Fire ONE real entry at the statistically derived n-1 reversion zone — the high-probability mean-reversion point. 
  
- The 5-bar breakout trigger is NOT arbitrary — it was derived from tick data backtesting and represents the empirically validated nth position in the virtual grid. 
  
**What is actually running now:** 
  
- The Hunter correctly fires a single entry at the probe-retraction point — this part works and the entries look visually clean after two weeks of demo. 
  
- The Watcher (ported from CAB philosophy) immediately contradicts the strategy — it kills positions with a 1.5–1.8×ATR reaper at precisely the moment the ghost grid says the trade needs room to find its reversion. 
  
- Two weeks of demo at 0.01 lot on XAUUSD with only $150 loss while running naked (no filters) is a meaningful survival signal for the entry model. 
  
**Why the current watcher is the wrong tool:** 
  
- A defensive single-trade watcher (CAB-style) is designed to minimise loss on individual positions. The ghost grid philosophy requires surviving through the accumulation zone — the exact adverse move the reaper is cutting. 
  
- The per-position reaper has no knowledge of the ghost grid state. It does not know whether price at -1.8×ATR represents a genuine failure or the approach of the next virtual grid layer (which may signal imminent exhaustion and reversal).

# **5. Phase 2 — Individual Conviction Testing** 
  
All three enriched bots run simultaneously on demo for 2 weeks. Each bot is evaluated independently against its own success criteria. This phase proves individual edge before any combination work begins. Bots that fail their conviction gate stay in Phase 2 for a further week with targeted adjustments. 
  
## **5.1 SuperTrend — Conviction Criteria** 
  
| **Metric** | **Gate to Pass** | **Action if Failed** | 
| --- | --- | --- | 
| **Positive expectancy** | Avg P&L per trade > 0 after spread across ≥ 30 trades | Review SI weight calibration per symbol | 
| **SI vs outcome correlation** | High-SI entries (>0.65) win rate > low-SI (<0.50) by 15%+ | Re-examine cluster weight and volume weight | 
| **Equity filter activations** | < 3 activations per week (filter working, not over-sensitive) | Adjust min_ratio or filter period | 
| **Cache efficiency** | Cache hit rate > 90% (logged on shutdown) | Investigate if timeframe setting is correct | 
| **Watcher state distribution** | CONFIRMED > 40% of managed bars across all trades | Review incubation_bars settings | 
| **Failover test** | Manually kill Python — MQL5 EA takes over within 120s | Fix before any further testing | 
  
## **5.2 CAB Watcher — Conviction Criteria** 
  
| **Metric** | **Gate to Pass** | **Action if Failed** |

# **6. Phase 3 — Cross-Validation ****&**** Pre-Combination** 
  
Before combining bots into one process, this phase runs all three enriched bots simultaneously and validates that their regime activations are genuinely complementary in practice — not just in theory. Duration: 1 week. 
  
**Key observations to record during Phase 3:** 
  
- Track regime classification timestamps across all three bots — confirm that SuperTrend TRENDING entries and Ghost Grid RANGING entries do not overlap in time more than 10% of cases. 
  
- Track total daily P&L across all three bots combined — does the portfolio smooth out compared to individual bots? 
  
- Record any instances where all three bots are simultaneously in active positions — this represents full capital deployment and should be examined for correlation risk. 
  
- Confirm the MQL5 watchdog handles all three bots' magic numbers correctly under failover. 
  
- Record CPU and RAM usage with three separate Python processes as baseline for comparison against unified architecture. 
  
**Decision gate to exit Phase 3:** 
  
- All three bots individually passed their Phase 2 conviction criteria. 
  
- Combined portfolio shows lower peak drawdown than any individual bot alone. 
  
- Regime overlap (all three active simultaneously) < 15% of total trading time. 
  
- No regime conflict observed — SuperTrend and Ghost Grid never opening opposing positions on the same instrument. 
  
# **7. Phase 4 — Unified Architecture** 
  
One Python process. One MT5 connection. One regime classifier. Three strategy engines activated by regime. This phase begins only after all three bots have individually proven their edge in Phases 2 and 3. 
  
## **7.1 Architecture Layers** 
  
| **Layer** | **Name** | **Responsibility** | 
| --- | --- | --- | 
| **0** | **Shared Intelligence** | Fetch M1/M15/M30/H4 data once. Compute regime classifier, conviction, ATR, H4 breakout detector. All engines read from shared state object — no duplicate API calls. | 
| **1** | **Regime Router** | One decision per cycle: RANGING → Ghost Grid active; TRENDING → SuperTrend active; EXHAUSTION → CAB Harvester; H4 break → structural bias applied. Replaces three separate regime classifiers. | 
| **2** | **Strategy Engines** | SuperTrend engine (K-Means, M30 entry), Ghost Grid engine (probe detect, ghost cache, M1), CAB engine (Harvester/Protector/Reaper). Each engine receives shared state from Layer 0 — no own data fetch. | 
| **3** | **Unified Watcher** | Per-magic dispatch: 201→scalp-BE, 202→grid-manager, 203→ATR-trail, ST_MAGIC→SI state machine, CAB_MAGIC→Harvester-Protector. One watcher loop manages all positions from all strategies. | 
| **4** | **Risk Orchestration** | Global position cap across all strategies. Equity curve filter (unified). Per-strategy max positions. Symbol isolation. Session gate applied globally. |

# **8. Risk Management Framework** 
  
These rules apply across all phases and all bots. They are non-negotiable constraints, not suggestions. 
  
## **8.1 Capital Exposure Rules** 
  
- No bot moves to live capital until it has passed its Phase 2 conviction gate. 
  
- Live deployment begins at micro-lot sizing (0.01) for a minimum 2-week live test before scaling. 
  
- The gap between demo and live is largest for Ghost Grid (M1, execution-sensitive) and smallest for SuperTrend (M30, slower signal). Plan for more live micro-lot test time for Ghost Grid. 
  
- Maximum combined drawdown per day across all bots: define this number before Phase 2 begins and enforce it mechanically, not discretionally. 
  
## **8.2 Ghost Grid Specific Hard Rules** 
  
- Hard grid depth cap: maximum 4 real entries per grid. Non-negotiable. A trending day without this cap converts accumulation into geometric loss. 
  
- Grid stop-loss: defined as 3× first layer risk. If combined grid P&L reaches this level, close all positions immediately. 
  
- Minimum ATR reset before re-arming: after a grid closes at stop-loss, require price to move at least 1 full ATR before arming the next grid. 
  
- Do not increase grid depth cap or widen grid stop during demo testing. These are the parameters that keep a grid strategy survivable. Evaluate them statistically after 50+ grids, not impressionistically. 
  
## **8.3 Live Deployment Sequencing** 
  
| **Order** | **Bot** | **Go-live condition** | **Scaling condition** |
